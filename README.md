## Maryland State Coastal Wetland Data Inventory

Coastal wetlands serve as important carbon sinks and provide valuable ecosystem services, yet the data needed to quantify and manage these ecosystems are often unevenly distributed and inaccessible to the public. The Coastal Carbon Network maintains and hosts a publicly available synthesis of carbon data from coastal wetland ecosystems. In this presentation, the availability and representation of wetland data across Maryland is examined at a sub-state level, focusing on watershed-level patterns in data quantity, type, utility, habitat representation, and spatial distribution. We assessed where existing datasets provide coverage of MD coastal wetland habitat and identify potential geographic and habitat-specific gaps in publicly available data. Additionally, we synthesized available soil carbon data to visualize carbon stocks across MD watersheds. By examining data availability alongside carbon stocks, this presentation highlights both areas that are well-represented in existing datasets and where additional data collection or publication could improve our understanding of MD coastal carbon resources. This assessment emphasizes how publicly accessible data inventories can identify opportunities to strengthen regional datasets, support future carbon modeling efforts, and improve the accessibility of coastal wetland data for researchers, decision-makers, and community members.
